## Summary

Optional add-on for World PvP Police Guild: your guild's calls for help, "On my way" answers and
"All clear" go to its Discord, where the World PvP Police bot turns them into cards.

## Description

This one only makes sense if your guild runs the World PvP Police bot on its Discord. Without the
bot, World PvP Police Guild on its own does the whole job in game, and you can skip this.

With it installed, a call you send after a ganking is also written to your guild's Discord through
the game's own Discord link. The bot picks that line up and posts a card in the channel your
officers chose: the zone map with a dot where you died, who killed you, your message, and a ping
for the role they set. Guildmates who click "On my way" in game get added to the card, and "All
clear" closes it. Someone who isn't logged in can see the fight happening and decide to log on.

The guild needs to be linked to a Discord server, with Discord chat kept separate from guild chat.
The guild leader or an officer sets that up in Guild & Communities (right-click the guild, its
Discord settings). /wpp discord tells you whether your guild is linked. When it isn't, the call box
says so, and hovering that line lists what's missing. The call still goes out in game either way.

Guild on merged chat? /wpp fusion on writes your calls into guild chat as well, so they reach
Discord that way. The whole guild sees those lines in game, which is why it's off by default. The
same switch is a box on the World PvP Police Guild settings page.

Requires World PvP Police Guild. The bot is free and open source:
https://github.com/Wafhi3n/WorldPvPPoliceBot (a guild hosts its own copy).

Made for WoW: Forever. This is a first beta and it's only been tried on a test client. English,
French, German and Spanish.
