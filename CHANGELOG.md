# Changelog - World PvP Police Guild - Discord

## v0.1.0-beta - 2026-11-18

First beta, made for WoW: Forever. It's only been tried on a test client so far.

- Writes your World PvP Police calls, the "On my way" answers and the "All clear" to your guild's
  Discord, through the game's own Discord link. The World PvP Police bot turns each call there into
  a card with the zone map and who's coming.
- The guild has to be linked to a Discord server with Discord chat kept separate from guild chat
  (a guild leader or officer setting). `/wpp discord` tells you where the link stands. When it
  isn't linked, hover the "Discord: not linked" line in the call box to see what's missing.
- Guild on merged chat? `/wpp fusion on` writes your calls into guild chat instead, which the whole
  guild sees. It's also a box on the addon's settings page.
- Needs World PvP Police Guild. No bot on your guild's Discord means you don't need this addon.
- English, French, German and Spanish.
